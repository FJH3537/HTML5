Green Technology 101

What is green technology?

Green technology uses scientific knowledge, engineering, and responsible design to reduce environmental damage, conserve natural resources, and support a sustainable quality of life.

A Simple Definition

Technology designed with the planet in mind

It is not one single invention. Green technology is a broad group of products, systems, and practices that prevent pollution, use energy and materials efficiently, and replace harmful processes with cleaner alternatives.

01 — PREVENT

Reduce harm at the source

Cleaner production avoids waste and pollution before they enter the environment.

02 — CONSERVE

Use fewer resources

Efficient systems provide the same service while consuming less energy, water, and raw material.

03 — RESTORE

Support natural systems

Sustainable design helps ecosystems recover and remain resilient for future generations.

Where It Appears

Green technology in everyday life

These solutions work across energy, cities, agriculture, water, transport, and material use.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hydro, and other renewable sources produce power with lower emissions.

Energy Efficiency

Smart grids, storage, and efficient devices reduce unnecessary energy consumption.

Water Management

Monitoring, treatment, and reuse technologies protect clean water and reduce waste.

Smart Green Cities

Efficient buildings, public transport, and urban greenery make cities more livable.

Why It Matters

Benefits beyond the environment

Well-designed green technology can address environmental, economic, and social needs at the same time.

Lower pollution and greenhouse-gas emissions

Cleaner energy and production methods improve environmental quality and public health.

Greater efficiency and long-term savings

Using less electricity, fuel, water, and material can reduce operating costs over time.

More resilient communities

Local clean energy, better infrastructure, and responsible resource management help communities adapt to change.
