Join Our Mission

Become a volunteer and help us sort, repair,
and recycle e-waste for a greener future.

Volunteer Roles

Tech Sorting

Help us categorize incoming e-waste into working, repairable, and scrap piles. No technical experience required!

Logistics Assistant

Assist during our drop-off drives. You will help greet donors, carry boxes, and organize the storage units.

Hardware Repair

Have experience fixing PCs, laptops, or phones? Join our specialized team to refurbish tech for low-income students.

Perks: All volunteers will receive a Green Tech Club t-shirt and USSDC points!
