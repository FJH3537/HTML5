One city, connected systems

Explore Smart-City Systems

A smart city is not a single device. It combines people, sensors, public services and responsible decision-making.

Efficient infrastructure

Smart energy management

Meters and building controls reveal when electricity is used, helping facility teams reduce waste and shift demand away from peak periods.

Data collected

Smart meters record electricity demand across buildings and public infrastructure.

City response

Building controls adjust lighting, cooling and battery use according to current demand.

Expected result

The city uses energy more efficiently and reduces unnecessary peak demand.

Balanced development

Benefits and challenges

Smart-city projects can improve urban life, but cities must plan for cost, maintenance, privacy and equal access.

Benefits Challenges
More efficient energy use High initial implementation cost
Lower transport emissions Ongoing maintenance and technical skills
Faster detection of environmental problems Privacy and cybersecurity risks
Faster, data-informed public services Unequal digital access
