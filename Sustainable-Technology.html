Clean energy

Solar microgrids

Local solar panels, batteries and controls work together to keep essential spaces powered efficiently.

Best for: Managing a local electricity network and maintaining essential power.

Challenge: High initial investment and ongoing battery maintenance.

Clean energy

Smart energy storage

Digital controls store renewable electricity when supply is high and release it when demand increases.

Best for: Saving electricity for later and reducing peak demand.

Challenge: Battery cost, ageing, temperature control and end-of-life handling.

Resources

Smart water monitoring

Connected meters and leak sensors reveal unusual water use before a small problem becomes major waste.

Challenge: Sensors require correct placement, calibration and maintenance.

Resources

Food-waste anaerobic digestion

A sealed digester uses microorganisms to turn separated food waste into biogas and nutrient-rich digestate.

Challenge: Feedstock contamination and process conditions must be controlled.

Agriculture

Precision greenhouses

Sensors adjust watering, temperature and light so plants receive what they need with fewer resources.

Challenge: Equipment and energy use can be costly without careful management.

Green infrastructure

Nature-based design

Green roofs, shade trees and rain gardens cool built areas while supporting biodiversity and drainage.

Challenge: Plant selection and long-term upkeep must suit the site.

Suggested starting point

Solar microgrid pilot

Measure one building's daily demand, then size a small solar and battery demonstration around its essential loads.

First action: Review three months of electricity bills and identify peak-use periods.

From concept to project

A simple innovation pathway

Good green technology begins with a clearly defined need and improves through testing, evidence and feedback.

Observe the problem

Identify who is affected, where it happens and what resource is being wasted.

Build a small pilot

Test one measurable solution before expanding it across the campus.

Measure and improve

Compare results, collect user feedback and refine the design before scaling up.
